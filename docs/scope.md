# Study objectives and available evidence

The report's objectives on PDF pages 3-4 are to georeference an image, map habitats and site features, and produce terrain-related maps. The supplied report includes resulting figures, but original imagery, control points, vector layers, and elevation rasters were not supplied.

Use the report to understand the intended workflow and its reported outputs. Do not treat the presence of a map as independent proof of its positional accuracy or habitat-classification quality. Those claims need the relevant source records and checks.

This edition includes a small synthetic area-summary example to demonstrate data handling. It is not a field survey, a new habitat assessment, or a reproduction of the farm's mapped areas.
