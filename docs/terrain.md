# Terrain provenance and interpretation gaps

PDF pages 7-9 describe SRTM elevation data and kriging in relation to terrain maps. Before reproducing the workflow, identify the exact raster product, tile, resolution, vertical units, resampling choices, and whether interpolation was actually applied to points or another intermediate dataset.

For each derived output, record its input surface and settings. Keep slope, aspect, and shaded-relief interpretation separate rather than inferring physical meaning merely from bright or dark colours. The report's descriptions need to be reconciled with the original project and legend.

A later comparison should hold the study extent and inputs constant when changing a processing choice. The supplied PDF alone does not support a numerical statement about interpolation error or terrain accuracy, and no such result is claimed here.
